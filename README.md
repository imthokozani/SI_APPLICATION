# SI_APPLICATION
**Overview**

The SI Leader application is designed to reduce the amount of administrative work that Supplemental Instruction (SI) Leaders have to perform during and after SI sessions.

SI Leaders often need to spend significant amounts of time recording learner attendance and maintaining administrative records. This can take time away from their primary responsibility of supporting learners during SI sessions.

The application aims to simplify this process by allowing learners to record their attendance digitally while automating the administrative work that would normally have to be completed manually by the SI Leader.

**The Problem**

During an SI session, the SI Leader needs to keep track of which learners attended the session and maintain the required attendance records.

A manual process can involve:

Recording learner attendance.
Collecting learner information.
Entering attendance information into an Excel spreadsheet.
Maintaining and updating administrative records.

When this process is repeated across multiple sessions, the administrative workload can become time-consuming.

**The Solution**

The application provides a digital attendance process that allows learners to record their own attendance.

The SI Leader generates a QR code for an SI session. Learners scan the QR code using their devices and are directed to an attendance form where they provide the required information and submit it.

Once the form is submitted, the application automatically processes the information and updates the relevant Excel spreadsheet.

This removes the need for the SI Leader to manually enter attendance information into the spreadsheet.

**How It Works**

The general process is:

The SI Leader creates an SI session and generates a QR code.
The QR code is made available to learners during the session.
Learners scan the QR code.
Learners complete the attendance form.
Learners submit the form.
The application processes the submitted information.
The relevant attendance information is automatically recorded in the Excel spreadsheet.
